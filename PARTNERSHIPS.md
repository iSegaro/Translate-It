# Partnerships

Translate It is open to partnerships that bring technology, services, infrastructure, or other benefits to the project and its users. Proposals are reviewed individually for usefulness, transparency, and alignment with the project's interests.

## Partnership scope

Examples include:

- AI and API providers
- cloud and infrastructure services
- developer tools
- browser and distribution platforms
- accessibility and education initiatives
- services or credits offered to Translate It users
- other relevant technical or ecosystem collaborations

This list is not exhaustive.

Collaboration may include integration or compatibility work, testing and technical feedback, documentation, credits or benefits for users, public acknowledgement, and collaboration on relevant technical work. The scope of each partnership is agreed separately.

## Independence

A partnership does not guarantee:

- integration into Translate It
- default provider placement
- higher ranking or visibility
- feature priority
- roadmap control
- exclusivity

Technical and product decisions remain independent and are based on what is appropriate for the project and its users.

## Transparency

Commercial relationships that are directly relevant to the product will be disclosed where appropriate.

For example, if a provider supplies credits, infrastructure, or support for an integration, that relationship may be noted in the relevant documentation or project materials.

A partnership or commercial relationship does not mean that Translate It endorses a provider, service, or product.
